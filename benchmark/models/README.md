# Benchmark models

[Türkçe](README.tr.md)

Test parts for the insert-mode study (test plan: T1–T12). All files are generated by `generate_models.py` from the parameters at the top of the script, so the exact geometry can be rebuilt and cited.

```
pip install manifold3d trimesh numpy
python generate_models.py
```

![Preview of all models](preview.png)

Every part is already standing in its print orientation (Z = print direction). **Seam Z** is where the print is stopped and the part is re-inserted: enter it as the part height in the insert tab. The last good layer at 0.20 mm is **seam Z / 0.2**. The wall column shows Layer Rescue's default wall height for that seam (clamp(Z/2, 3, 15), at most Z − 1).

## What each part is for

- **M1** – tensile bar with the ASTM D638 Type I outline (165 mm long, 13 mm × 7 mm gauge section, R76 fillets; 7 mm is the thickest Type I allows and keeps the tall bar from swaying), printed standing up so the seam lies across the load in the middle of the gauge length. It is tall and narrow: print it with a 10 mm brim and low accelerations.
- **M2** – 80 × 10 × 5 mm flexural bar, printed standing up, seam at mid-span. ISO 178 uses 4 mm; 5 mm is used because insert mode needs a narrow side of at least 5 mm. Report this deviation.
- **M3** – 20 × 20 × 30 mm block for a simple shear check across the seam.
- **G1–G7** – alignment parts. Each has four reference ribs (1.0 mm wide, 0.8 mm high, 90° apart) running through the seam, and a 0.4 mm deep groove 2 mm below the seam as a Z reference. The step on each rib at the seam gives the XY offset and rotation (see T1). G1 square, G2 cylinder, G3a/G3b cones that widen upwards (10° and 25° per side, base Ø16), G4 tube, G5 L-section, G6 thin part at the software limits (5 mm narrow side, 3 mm insert height), G7 = G1 with a 0° ledge (+X) and a 45° overhang (−X) above the seam for support reprinting.

## Notes

- Use the parameters of the test plan (0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 100 % infill for M parts) and keep slicer settings fixed across groups.
- The groove reduces the cross-section slightly at Z = seam − 2 mm; it is only on the G parts, never on the mechanical specimens.
- Label each printed part with its specimen ID (for example `T1-G1-c010-02`) with a marker or Bambu Studio's text tool on a surface away from the ribs.
